## Done: Phase 6 + 7
- Migration 0006 (tested): `set_order_status(order, status, note, actor, notify)` writes history note/actor, queues customer email per status, restocks on cancelled/returned/failed via idempotent `restock_order`, marks COD paid on delivered. `mark_order_paid` (idempotent, amount check, confirms pending orders, queues payment_received), `mark_payment_failed`, `switch_to_cod`.
